Support

Help with BikeStable for iPhone.

Need help with BikeStable? Review the answers below or contact us by email. Please do not include sensitive personal information or complete invoice images in your message.

Contact support

Email bikestablesupport@gmail.com for assistance. We aim to respond within two business days.

Where is my data stored?

BikeStable stores your bicycle, ride, service, and parts records on your iPhone. No BikeStable account or cloud database is required.

How do I export a service history?

Open a bicycle in BikeStable, choose its service-history export option, select the details to include, and create a PDF that you can save or share.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records or bicycles from within the app. Because BikeStable is local-first, deleting the app also removes its locally stored data unless it remains in an iPhone backup.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the restore-purchases option in BikeStable with the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Apple manages payment details and purchase processing.
