Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

BikeStable is designed to help you maintain a history for your bicycles. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

BikeStable stores the bicycle details, bike photos, rides and mileage, service records, line items, notes, costs, and invoice photos that you choose to add. You control the content entered into the app.

Local-first storage

BikeStable does not require an account or synchronize your records with a BikeStable cloud database. Your bicycle details, bike photos, rides and mileage, service records, line items, notes, costs, and invoice photos remain on your device and are subject to your device backup and security settings.

Invoice photos

You may attach an invoice photo from your camera or photo library to a service record. Invoice photos are stored locally on your device. BikeStable does not perform OCR or other text extraction on them, upload them, or use them for advertising.

Support and feedback

If you use the in-app Support & Feedback form, BikeStable sends your selected category, message, app version and build, device model, and iOS version to Formspree. You may also provide an email address if you would like a response; an email address is required when you select Get Help.

This information is used for support and app-functionality purposes, including responding to requests, investigating problems, and improving support. A submission may be linked to you when it includes your email address. BikeStable never uses support submissions for advertising or tracking. Formspree processes and retains submissions under its privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional BikeStable Premium is a lifetime, non-consumable in-app purchase sold through Apple. Apple processes the purchase and related transaction history. BikeStable uses RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage the Premium entitlement. RevenueCat processes purchase history, an anonymous App User ID, and Premium entitlement status for receipt validation, purchase restoration, feature access, and RevenueCat purchase analytics. BikeStable does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, or a BikeStable account, and does not link the anonymous App User ID to your real-world identity. BikeStable never uses purchase information for tracking.

RevenueCat does not receive your bicycle history, mileage, notes, bike photos, or invoice images. Those records stay on your device. Purchase information is handled according to Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

How information is used

Information handled by BikeStable is used to:

  • Display and maintain your bicycle history.
  • Generate reports and backups that you choose to export.
  • Unlock Premium features after a successful purchase or restore.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback that you submit.

Backups, reports, and sharing

You may create a full garage backup containing your bicycles, photos, rides and mileage, service records, line items, notes, costs, and invoice photos. You may also create reports from selected records. BikeStable only sends these files to a destination when you choose to export or share them. After export, you control the files and the services or people with whom you share them may apply their own privacy practices.

Analytics, advertising, tracking, and sale of data

BikeStable does not include advertising SDKs or general app-behavior analytics, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information. RevenueCat provides purchase analytics using the purchase information described above. BikeStable does not use bicycle or service records for analytics or targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app and its data. Deleting a saved invoice, service record, or bicycle also removes its associated local invoice files as applicable. Backups and reports you export are controlled by the destination where you save or share them.

Formspree support submissions and Apple or RevenueCat purchase records are retained under those providers’ policies and applicable legal requirements. To ask about a support submission or make a privacy request, email bikestablesupport@gmail.com. Apple may retain transaction records that BikeStable cannot delete.

Security

Information stored locally is protected by the security controls and settings available on your device. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as BikeStable develops. The revision date above will change when material updates are published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email bikestablesupport@gmail.com or visit the BikeStable support page.
